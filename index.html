My Favorite Spots in Chinatown

Where I Go

  • Nai Ge (restaurant): my go-to for sauerkraut fish
  • 10 Second (restaurant): rice noodles
  • Toro (restaurant): Japanese food
  • Kajiken (restaurant): Japanese food
  • Dynasty and Empire Garden (dim sum): my hidden gems
  • HeyTea, Molly Tea, Teazzi, and Tsaocaa (tea shops)
  • Corner Bakery (bakery)

How Much I Usually Spend

Each bar shows the midpoint of what I usually spend per person, and the dark line shows the full range. Nai Ge is usually shared between two people, so its price is split per person.

Looking at my spending, Chinatown is easier on the wallet than I expected. All four tea shops and the bakery cost $15 or less, so a quick drink or snack is an easy treat. The noodle and Japanese spots sit in the middle at around $15 to $30. Dim sum at Dynasty and Empire Garden is the most expensive at $30 to $40 per person, which makes it more of a weekend outing than an everyday meal.
