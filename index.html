Eating Through Boston's Chinatown

I visited Boston's Chinatown for the first time in September 2025, right after I moved to Boston for college. Before that, I spent my first year studying in London, where Chinatown in Soho became one of my regular spots. Whenever I missed home or just wanted a good meal, that was where I ended up. So when I arrived in a new city, finding Chinatown was one of the first things I did.

Honestly, Boston's Chinatown is smaller than I expected, and the variety of Chinese food is not as wide as what I was used to in London. But it quickly became my comfort zone anyway. It is where I go for a bowl of sauerkraut fish, a plate of rice noodles, or a slow weekend dim sum. It is not only Chinese food, either. There are great Japanese restaurants, plus bakeries and a growing number of tea shops that make it easy to stop by for a drink anytime.

On this website, I want to share the places that make Chinatown feel like home to me: the restaurants I keep going back to, the hidden gems that are easy to walk past, and the bakeries and tea shops I visit the most. Head over to My Favorite Spots to see my list and how I rate each place.
